A rock song built entirely through creative direction, not performance. No instrument was played and no lyric was written by hand. Instead, the entire song was designed on paper first: genre, structure, tempo, instrumentation, and emotional arc, then handed to an AI music tool to generate.

## Overview

I've been a fan of 2000s post-grunge and alt-rock for years (think Daughtry, Hinder). I wanted to actually make something inside that genre, so instead of learning guitar or production, I treated the songwriting process as a direction-writing exercise. I broke the genre down into its core ingredients and wrote out exactly what the track needed, structurally and sonically, then used [Soundverse AI](https://www.linkedin.com/company/soundverseai/) to generate the track from that direction.

## Design Choices, and Why

| Element | Choice | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Genre | 2000s post-grunge / alt-rock | Long-time genre interest, wanted something authentic to it |
| Reference artists | Daughtry, Hinder | Anchors the AI to a recognizable sonic era instead of generic "rock" |
| Tempo | ~100 BPM | Mid-tempo groove typical of the genre, not too fast, not a ballad |
| Structure | Short breakdown before verse → stripped-back verse → heavier chorus | Mirrors the genre's classic dynamic build, tension then release |
| Guitars | Palm-muted riffs swelling into power chords | Core sonic signature of post-grunge |
| Vocals | Clean, melodic, introspective | Matches the genre's emotional, moody delivery over screamed or aggressive vocals |

## How It Was Made

1. Researched and internalized the sonic traits of 2000s post-grunge (guitar tone, tempo range, vocal style, song dynamics).
2. Translated those traits into a structured, descriptive prompt covering genre, tempo, instrumentation, vocal style, and song arc.
3. Fed the prompt into Soundverse AI to generate the track.
4. Listened back and compared the result against the original creative intent.
